THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROFILE

Beyond the Credentials.

A journey shaped by curiosity, competition, creativity and the desire to build something of my own.

01 / THE BEGINNING

The Curious Child

Growing up, I was primarily focused on academics. Outside the classroom, I found my creative side through drawing, painting and video games. I loved sketching anime characters, particularly Goku, and spent countless hours bringing characters to life on paper.

I also explored Taekwondo, cricket and badminton. Badminton brought out my competitive side, and I participated in local competitions. Looking back, these early interests were my first experiences with creativity, exploration and competition.

Creativity Art Competition

02 / THE TURNING POINT

Football Changed Me

I started playing football at around 14, and honestly, I was terrible at first. I was often placed in goal, a position I didn't even enjoy. But something about the game made me want to improve.

I trained alongside players who were considerably older than me, some already 18, 19 and 20. Through consistent effort, I gradually improved and eventually earned a place in the first team.

Football taught me something that academics alone never had: the power of a growth mindset. Being bad at something didn't mean I had to remain bad at it. Discipline, smart work and persistence could change that.

Discipline Growth Mindset Leadership

03 / FINDING MY DIRECTION

Discovering Business

I chose Science with PCMB in Classes XI and XII because I wanted to keep my options open. I enjoyed learning science, but gradually found myself drawn towards a different world: business and entrepreneurship.

Shark Tank, business stories and the way companies operate sparked my curiosity. I became fascinated by the idea of building something of my own, making decisions and creating value independently. I found freedom in Business.

That interest eventually led me towards management education and IIM Bangalore. My experience in EY CAFTA further strengthened my interest in consulting and solving real-world business problems.

Entrepreneurship Consulting Problem Solving

04 / HOW I THINK

My Way of Moving Forward

I would describe myself through three qualities: instinct, adaptability and ambition.

Football taught me to read situations quickly, trust my instincts and make decisions under pressure. Over time, I've found myself applying that same instinct-led approach to business and entrepreneurial challenges.

My approach to effort is also influenced by Nishkam Karma: focusing on my actions and responsibilities rather than becoming consumed by the outcome. I believe in giving my best to the work itself and allowing the results to follow.

Instinct Adaptability Ambition

05 / LOOKING AHEAD

The Builder's Mindset.

I want to build a career around solving meaningful problems, understanding businesses and creating something that has an impact. Whether that means growing into a leadership role in a high-calibre organisation or eventually building a venture of my own, I want to keep learning, experimenting and taking on challenges that push me forward.
